### Background

A quick vegan protein shake for easy protein intake.

### Ingredients

- 25g pea protein
- 1 tsp cocoa powder
- 2 tsp fructose powder
- Plant alternative milk

## Directions

1. Add the pea protein, cocoa powder, and fructose powder to a shaker or blender.
2. Add plant alternative milk to your preferred thickness.
3. Shake or blend until smooth.
